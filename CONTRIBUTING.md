# Contributing

For attribution corrections, identify the prompt slug and link to its original source.

For translation review, open an issue with the language, page URL, message key, proposed wording, and reason. Prioritize natural phrasing, correct image-generation terms, and consistency of prices and credit terms. Preserve product names, URLs, numbers, and placeholders such as `{title}` and `{{date}}`.

The six language files in `translation-review/` include English context and proposed localized text. They are review drafts for application copy; their presence does not mean a native speaker has approved them or that they have been deployed. No paid review is commissioned through this repository.
